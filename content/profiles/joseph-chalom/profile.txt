Bio: Joseph Chalom is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Sharplink (Nasdaq: SBET), having joined the company in July 2025. He brings over 20 years of experience as Managing Director and Head of Strategic Ecosystem Partnerships at BlackRock. He was instrumental in launching some of the largest digital asset products, including the iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) which became the largest Bitcoin ETF globally, and the iShares Ethereum Trust (ETHA), the leading spot Ethereum ETF in the U.S., and BUIDL, the first tokenized U.S. Treasury fund built on Ethereum.
